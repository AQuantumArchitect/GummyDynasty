## Large-scale abstraction (research, post-delivery)

The 1,000 orange dots are a sheet of non-colliding balls. Fine as a far-field hint. Wrong up close. Not an abstraction — just cheaper drawing.

Later work, in this order:

1. **Why a crowd is not a particle sheet** — occupancy, packing, local avoidance, or a density field that can push and be pushed.
2. **M5 ladder** — refine a ghost into a hopper and collapse it back. Population, faction, COM/momentum, intent ancestry, latent mix (wounded / scared / scattered) must survive.
3. **M6 attention** — only the region that matters is physical. Cheap march → inbound rock → refine → settle → cheap again.
4. **M7 crowd variables** — density, COM, cohesion, pressure. Pick the cheapest model that still reconstructs.
5. Collision that *looks* like gummies from far away (impostors, capsules, or a soft occupancy grid) before any instanced-sphere army ships as a feature.

Keys `5` / `6` / `F9` stay in the toy as a research hatch. They are not the product.

## Playtest cases parked for Wave R

These showed up while playing the current toybox. Do not start them now. They are the right problems once phones and showcases have been laughed at.

1. **March leftovers still slide after plant.** Hold now latches and slots sit inside the victory band, but hoppers on grass still keep a bit of horizontal drift. A cheap settle / attention collapse of a planted file is the research version of this, not another hop retune.
2. **Candy flinch is local, not a crowd.** Incoming stones now write threat on whoever they are about to hit. A packed file should *feel* a wave of dodges, not five independent flinches. That is a crowd-field case (M7), with "one stone, one file" as the comparison.
3. **Train is a moving relevance region.** Cars, wall, flag, and hoppers now ride together as toys. The honest research question is: the three cars far from the camera should get cheaper, then refine when a stone is in the air or a hopper falls between cars.
4. **Castle keep stacks sit idle.** North/south/west crates are scenery until something hits them. A formation entering the courtyard is the moment a wall should become physical-attention-expensive. Right now every crate is born expensive.
5. **Phone commander sees COM + wall + flag, not beliefs.** Fine for a friend. Later the same surface should show the file's tactic and a cheap occupancy hint so a stranger understands *why* they spread.
6. **DROP + LOB + X at the same breach.** Best physical story in the pit. Best refinement-storm candidate (Benchmark D) when R starts: one event, many bodies, then settle.
7. **A one-crate slit is not a door, but a flopping 5-body gummy might still squeeze.** The lab now refuses to call that air a hole. Wave R occupancy should decide whether a soft body may deform through a 3 cm press-fit or must pile up. Do not retune hop to fake this.
